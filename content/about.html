Who We Are

About This Guide

We are a small team of expedition travellers and polar research enthusiasts who believe that choosing the right South Georgia small ship cruise should be straightforward — not an exercise in deciphering marketing copy.

Our Mission

South Georgia is one of the most extraordinary places on Earth. Reaching it requires a significant investment of time and money — and the operator and ship you choose will determine whether your voyage is a life-changing expedition or a frustrating exercise in waiting aboard while others go ashore.

This site was created to provide a genuinely independent, methodologically transparent ranking of South Georgia small ship cruise operators. We evaluate each operator against 6 objective criteria, update our rankings quarterly, and accept no payment from any operator for their ranking position.

We have combined experience of over 40 expedition cruise voyages across the subantarctic and polar regions, including multiple South Georgia voyages on four of the operators we rank. Where we have not personally travelled with an operator, we draw on verified traveller reviews from IAATO-monitored databases, operator transparency reports, and direct consultation with expedition guides.

Editorial Principles

◆
No paid placements.

Operators cannot pay to improve their position in our rankings. Rankings are determined solely by our 6-point methodology.

◆
Quarterly price verification.

We verify all pricing data each quarter directly from operator websites and booking systems. Outdated prices are corrected immediately.

◆
Transparent methodology.

Our complete ranking criteria, weights, and data sources are published on our Editorial Policy page. We explain every ranking decision.

◆
Corrections welcomed.

If you spot an error — pricing, ship specifications, or factual inaccuracies — please contact us. We publish corrections within 48 hours.

Our 6-Point Rating Methodology

We evaluate each South Georgia small ship cruise operator against 6 criteria. Full criterion definitions and scoring rubrics are on the Editorial Policy page.

1
Itinerary Quality Coverage of key South Georgia landing sites; schedule flexibility; Falklands combination quality
25%
2
Wildlife Landing Access Time ashore per day; IAATO compliance; rotation requirements based on ship size
25%
3
Expedition Guiding Naturalist-to-passenger ratio; lecturer credentials; onboard educational programme quality
20%
4
Ship Comfort & Size Cabin quality; public space ratio; stabilizer systems; all-suite vs. mixed cabin categories
15%
5
Sustainability Practices IAATO compliance level; B Corp / third-party certifications; environmental programme quality
10%
6
Value for Money Price-to-experience ratio; what you get per dollar spent relative to market alternatives
5%
